## Lessons (session 6)
- Enterprise Competency Proficiency Levels add a second assessment dimension beyond the Job Leveling Framework's Scope/Complexity/Impact. The JLF tells you "what level of work" someone does; the competency matrix tells you "how they do it" (Multiplier behaviors, strategic thinking, influence patterns). Combining both produces richer assessments than either alone - concrete application of the "multi-source framework synthesis" concept.
- Disconfirmation gates are most valuable when the initial assessment is positive. Without one, the Juanje assessment would have leaned "almost ready for promotion" based on the number of Level 5 behaviors. The gate surfaced important counterpoints: Impact still functional/tactical, external visibility missing, cross-org networks emerging not established, consistency over multiple quarters unproven.
