## Lessons

- The official RH release attributes go far beyond technical quality - 19 dimensions spanning QE, CEE, PXE, Product Security, Legal, Sales, and Product Management. The current RHAS criteria cover roughly 4 of 19 dimensions (feature status, product quality, production use, partially security).
- Key insight: the biggest RHAS TP gaps are not technical but organizational - no QE lead to sign off (PITCREW-337), no SBR/support readiness, no feedback collection mechanism (which is the entire purpose of Tech Preview per the official definition).
