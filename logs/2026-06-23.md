  1. **Zero-tickets heuristic** — the disconfirmation gate flags when a team shows 0 closed tickets vs 3+ in the previous report, because sudden drops to zero more likely indicate a Jira fetch failure than a team doing nothing.

## Lessons (session 11)

- **Permissions as design applies to outbound actions.** The Slack posting incident demonstrated why deny-by-default matters: the skill should never have included an outbound Slack posting step without explicit user authorization. Reading Slack (data collection) and writing to Slack (posting) are fundamentally different permission levels — a skill that reads from a service should not assume it can also write to that service.
