Introduction

This document defines measurable pass/fail conditions for each RHAS release milestone. Updated Aug 23, 2026 to incorporate decisions from Aug 17 RHAS testing/Quality review meeting.

IMPORTANT: These criteria are recommendations based on RHIVOS CTC patterns, OpenShift Quality Strategy, and Rachel Sibley/Pablo Ridolfi requirements. PITCREW-337 (QE Readiness) is the official epic - currently unassigned with 0 comments after 3+ months. Formal adoption requires QE lead assignment and stakeholder approval.

Criteria Tiers:

  • Monthly Release (RHAS-MMYY): Baseline quality for monthly iterations
  • Tech Preview (RHAS-0926, Sep 29): Superset of Monthly + customer-facing readiness. Note: Downstream testing deferred to GA per Aug 17 decision
  • GA (RHAS-1226, Dec 22): Superset of Tech Preview + production-grade validation + downstream artifact testing
