# Competency Proficiency Levels by Job Levels

Source: Competency Proficiency Levels by Job Levels PDF, March 2022.

Enterprise competency expectations for each job level. Use alongside the Multiplier competencies in `../../qc-agent/reference/multiplier-competencies.md` for behavioral assessment.

## Proficiency definitions

**Knowledgeable** - Uses the behaviors to enhance personal work effectiveness.
- Demonstrates an understanding of the concept.
- Practices consistently across similar situations.

**Experienced** - Uses the behaviors in a way that contributes to the effectiveness of the team.
- Shares understanding with others.
- Practices across new or unfamiliar situations.

**Advanced** - Uses the behaviors in a way that contributes to the effectiveness of multiple teams or a department.
- Models and teaches to others.
- Demonstrates depth and breadth of knowledge in applying concept.

**Expert** - Uses the behaviors in a way that contributes to the effectiveness of the company.
- Builds through programs, processes, and systems.
- Is recognized as an authority or thought leader in area of expertise.

### Key observations for IC assessment

- **Level 3 (Senior):** All competencies at Experienced. This is the "team contributor" level - behaviors contribute to team effectiveness.
- **Level 4 (Principal):** Execution, Problem Solving, and Customer Focus jump to Advanced. These three competencies differentiate the Principal level - they should demonstrate department-level impact in execution and problem-solving.
- **Level 5 (Sr Principal):** Red Hat Multiplier, Strategic, Influence, Team Advocate, and Continuous Learning rise to Advanced. At this level, ALL competencies are at least Advanced - cross-team/department impact across the board.
- **Execution and Problem Solving lead the curve** - they reach Advanced one level before most other competencies, and Expert one level before as well.

### Key observations for manager assessment

- **Associate Manager:** Starts at Experienced for most competencies (higher than IC Level 2). Strategic, Problem Solving, and Continuous Learning remain at Knowledgeable - growing areas for new managers.
- **Manager (M3):** All competencies at Experienced - consistent with the "team effectiveness" proficiency level. Strategic and Continuous Learning catch up.
- **Sr. Manager (M4):** Red Hat Multiplier, Strategic, Influence, Execution, and Continuous Learning jump to Advanced. Team Advocate, Problem Solving, and Customer Focus stay at Experienced - the differentiation is in strategic and influential leadership.
- **Team Advocate and Customer Focus lag** - they reach Advanced one level later than most other competencies for managers.

## Cross-track comparison

For the same Career Milestone Prefix (e.g., C6 = IC Prof Level 3 = Associate Manager):
- An IC Level 3 Senior is at Experienced across all competencies
- An Associate Manager starts at Experienced for most, but Knowledgeable for Strategic, Problem Solving, and Continuous Learning
- This suggests the IC-to-manager transition requires growing into Strategic and Continuous Learning competencies that the IC track developed earlier
