# Talent Cycle Guidance - July 2026

Source: Tashana's email "Upcoming Talent Cycle Guidance," July 8, 2026.

## Key changes

### Engineering Competencies retired

Engineering Competencies have been retired for most roles:
- Software Engineering
- Quality Engineering
- Engineering Managers
- Project Management - Technical

**What to use instead:** Assess performance against the **Job Descriptions** (Skill Progression Matrices) only. The Radford-based Job Leveling Framework (Scope, Complexity, Impact) remains valid as a cross-cutting lens, but behavioral competency assessment now lives inside the progression matrices.

**Exception:** Engineering Competencies remain active for **IC-6 (Distinguished Engineer)** and above.

**Impact on existing assessments:** Any prior assessment referencing Enterprise Competency Readiness tables (e.g., Red Hat Multiplier, Strategic, Influence, Execution, Team Advocate, Problem Solving, Customer Focus, Continuous Learning as separate competency dimensions) should be treated as supplementary context, not the formal framework. The Skill Progression Matrices are now the authoritative reference.

### High Potential Flag (replaces "Future Talent")

- Identifies **long-term potential**, not current high performance
- Reserved for **top 15%** to prioritize retention efforts
- Selecting someone is a **commitment to their growth** - the manager must:
  - Establish clear developmental actions
  - Provide exposure to higher-level roles
  - Widen their scope of influence across the org
  - Assign more complex responsibilities
- **Tell the associate** they are High Potential and discuss development plans
- References: High Potential Talent Reference Guide, curated KBA list (both linked from Tashana's email)

### Underperformance documentation

For associates not meeting expectations:
- **Don't wait** to address concerns
- Document feedback in **Workday AND email** ("if it's not in writing it doesn't really count")
- Template available (linked from Tashana's email)
- Ensure a **clear link between performance feedback and reward** (VIC, merit increase, equity)
- "One of the fastest ways to demotivate a team is the perception that low performance isn't being addressed"

### QE to SE transition path

- **Optional** - check QE interest during this talent cycle
- Focus on **career growth**, does not impact job security
- QE and SE job families **do not map 1:1** - transitioning often represents a **promotion** in both expectations and compensation (location-dependent)
- If interest noted in **Talent Action** section of Workday, Tashana can coordinate support and training resources

### Promotion requirements

- Work with teams to **clarify promotion requirements**
- See Promotion Manager Resources (linked from Tashana's email)
