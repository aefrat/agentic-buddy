# Talent Management for People Managers

Source: Red Hat internal "Talent Management for People Managers" Source page (6 tabs), saved July 12, 2026.

**When to read:** Running talent cycle (performance ratings, HiPo designation, movement readiness, promotion justification, talent actions, calibration). Reference for exact rating scales, definitions, eligibility criteria, and Workday visibility rules.

---

## 1. Overview - Talent Assessment Components

| Component | Questions | Visible to associate? |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------|
| Performance | 2 (What + How) | YES (after calibration) |
| High Potential | 2 (designator questions) | NO (may be discussed) |
| Movement Readiness | 1 | NO |
| Talent Actions | 1 | NO |
| Promotions | 4 (justification) | N/A (process) |

**Participation:** All full-time and part-time Red Hatters with 3+ months tenure. Excludes contractors, partner engineers, and new hires under 90 days.

---

## 2. Performance Ratings

Two dimensions rated independently, then combined:

**What** did the associate accomplish? (outcomes, impact on team and org)
**How** did the associate accomplish their goals? (Red Hat Multiplier behaviors)

### Rating scale (each dimension, 0-3)

| Score | Rating |
|-------|--------|
| 3 | Surpasses expectations |
| 2 | Meets expectations |
| 1 | Meets some expectations |
| 0 | Does not meet expectations |

### Combined performance rating (What + How = 0-6)

| Rating | Score | Definition |
|--------|-------|------------|
| High Impact Performer | 6 | Consistently surpassing all performance expectations for their role |
| Successful Performer | 4-5 | Consistently meeting expectations and at times surpassing some |
| Evolving Performer | 2-3 | Meeting some expectations but not all yet. Growth opportunities identified |
| Low Performer | 0-1 | Significant performance gaps, not meeting expectations |

### Distribution guidance (benchmark, not enforced)

| Tier | Target % |
|------|----------|
| High Impact | 10-20% |
| Successful | 60-80% |
| Evolving | 5-15% |
| Low | 2-5% |

Small teams (under 10) may have higher percentages. Ensure the rating aligns with what was discussed in the Quarterly Connection conversation.

Managers are expected to share ratings so the associate has a clear understanding of the rationale.

## 3. High Potential (HiPo) Talent

**Definition:** An individual with the aspiration, capability, engagement, and agility to rise to and succeed in higher-level roles or leadership positions with broader and deeper responsibilities, at a faster pace than their peers.

**Benchmark:** Up to 15% of the organization.

**Visibility:** NOT visible to associates in Workday. May be discussed between manager and associate, especially relating to talent actions and development activities.

### Four descriptors (ALL must be demonstrated)

| Descriptor | Definition | Observable indicators |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Aspiration** | Clear desire to advance and take on leadership or more impactful roles | Pursues increased scope; aims for influence; aptitude and desire to mentor others; job enjoyment; desires mobility; seeks visibility or recognition |
| **Capability** | Observable skills suggesting capacity for greater responsibilities | Cognitive: problem-solving, strategic thinking. Emotional: resilience, self-awareness, influencing. Interpersonal: building relationships, leading teams effectively |
| **Engagement** | Highly committed to Red Hat's mission, values, and objectives | Emotional commitment; going above and beyond; intent to stay and grow; volunteering extra energy outside role; positive attitude that inspires others |
| **Agility** | Can adapt quickly, learn from experiences, and lead through ambiguity or change effectively | How quickly and effectively someone can learn, adapt, and perform in new, complex, or changing situations. Reference: Red Hat Skills |

### Eligibility criteria

- Received a rating in the last two talent assessment cycles
- Typically assessed a High Impact performance rating and will continue performing at that level in the near-term
- Not on a performance improvement plan (PIP) or any behavioral improvement plans

## 4. Movement Readiness

Manager assesses associate's readiness to move to a new role within the next 6 months. NOT visible to associates.

### Definitions

| Rating | Definition | Criteria |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Continue growing in current role** | Focus on in-role development: stretch assignments, new skills, deepening expertise | Default |
| **Ready now to be promoted in current role (upcoming cycle)** | Nominated for in-role promotion during next compensation promotion cycle (budget dependent) | Must be High Impact Performer; 12-18 months at current level; role scope at next level per Job Leveling Framework; business need for next level |
| **Ready for lateral move outside of current role** | Ready for a new role outside current team at same level | Minimum Successful Performer; 12+ months in role; meets Internal Mobility Guidance |
| **Ready for promotion outside of current role** | Ready for promotion but no business need or role scope within current team | Minimum Successful Performer; 12+ months in role; meets Internal Mobility Guidance |
| **Not well placed** | Not thriving in current role; realignment may be needed | Typically Low/Evolving Performers; requires high-touch management |

## 5. Promotions

Only completed when Movement Readiness = "Ready Now to be promoted in current role (upcoming cycle)."

### Four justification items

1. **Proposed Job Profile Name** - job profile name and code after promotion. Reference: [Job Profiles and Codes in Workday](https://wd5.myworkday.com/redhat/d/task/1422$502.htmld)

2. **Business Need** - what changes in the function/org prompted the need? Long-term need for the role at next level? (Product shift, business growth, revenue priority)

3. **Expanded Role Scope** - new responsibilities and expectations. Alignment with next job level. Comparison with peers at that level.

4. **Associate Readiness** - 12-18 months in role? High Impact performer for at least one cycle? Rationale with examples.

### Promotion decision tree

```
1. Movement Readiness = "Ready Now to be promoted"?
   NO -> Not considered for promotion
   YES -> 2. Has associate demonstrated readiness?
          NO -> Change to "Continue growing in current role"
          YES -> 3. Business need and expanded role scope?
                 NO -> Change to "Ready for promotion outside of current role"
                 YES -> 4. Promotion recommendation approved?
                        NO -> Not approved (budget), keep "Ready Now"
                        YES -> Process in compensation cycle
```

### Eligibility guidance

- Minimum 12-18 months at current job level
- Promotion Eligibility & Increase Guidance: [Source page](https://source.redhat.com/career/forpeoplemanagers/people_manager_assets/people_managers_folder/promotions_eligibility__increase_guidance_qrg_for_managers_published_dec_2022pdf)
- [Promotions Reference Guide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1zFDGsdG2ULFi4wn0wWAUXS59TsbWgph5tiS3WB8ArSs/)

## 6. Talent Actions

Based on talent ratings, manager decides actions for the next 6 months. NOT visible to associates.

Source: [Talent Actions Guide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1aZLXZbT5cmmbk_UWRmF6KfLC5BG3ZyBszs1xCGgd8Bc/)

### Three categories

| Category | Key questions |
|----------|--------------|
| **Rewards** | Salary/OTE increase recommended? Equity recommendation based on role criticality, performance, future capability? |
| **Development** | How to support career aspirations? (Training, exposure, experiences.) Current gaps needing development plan? Would a PIP be appropriate? |
| **Growth** | Based on talent assessment, what should the associate focus on? |

### Rewards guidance by performance tier

| Tier | Salary | Equity/Cash Plan |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| High Impact | Greater rewards; more likely to receive | More likely to receive depending on role |
| Successful | Standard progression | Standard eligibility |
| Evolving | May not be selected to receive; or receive reduced rewards | May not be selected |
| Low | May not be selected to receive; or receive reduced rewards | May not be selected |
| High Potential (any tier) | May progress through salary range at accelerated rate | More likely to receive depending on role |

**Note on Bonus (VIC):** Use information from Quarterly Connection (not performance rating). Do NOT consider HiPo indicator for VIC - VIC is about contributions from previous quarter only.

### Development actions by performance tier

| Tier | Focus | Suggested activities |
|------|-------|---------------------|
| **High Impact** | Within-role development. Recognition, visibility to senior leadership, building/broadening expertise aligned to aspirations | Technical certifications/conferences (require investment), in-role project/initiative leadership, cross-functional projects, functional leader networking/exposure/mentoring, rotations |
| **Successful** | Within-role development with steady increase or shift of responsibilities. Coach and enable high performance | Experiences supporting mastering/broadening/deepening in-role learning, stretch projects/mentoring, team or community leader opportunities, communities of practice |
| **Evolving** | Within-role development to meet expectations. High-touch management with clearly documented onboarding/development plan, frequent 1:1s, coaching | Mentoring, Red Hat learning courses, targeted on-the-job exposure |
| **Low** | Partnership with People Relations for formal PIP or CAP. High-touch management with frequent 1:1s and coaching | PIP/CAP formal process |

These are not prescriptive - identify the most suited actions through conversations with associates.

### HiPo development requirements

Managers of HiPo associates **must**:
- Provide opportunities for greater responsibility / increase in scope, complexity, and impact within 12 months
- Provide opportunities to refine 1-2 high-value skills via targeted training, mentorship, coaching, and/or other development
- Work with the associate to select a **minimum of 2 high-touch activities** from:

| Activity | Purpose | Resources |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| **Rotational program or targeted development experience** | Broaden org acumen, develop versatility/adaptability, validate leadership capability, accelerate function expertise | Internal Mobility, Internal Gigs Program, Red Hat Learning Portal, Udemy, Red Hat Skills, Stretch Assignments Guidance, TTLA |
| **External/internal mentorship** | Knowledge transfer, diverse perspectives, career guidance, enhance engagement | Red Hat Careers Mentoring Program, Mentoring Program Reading List, Red Hat Mentoring Guide, Red Hat Skills Mentoring Program |
| **External/internal speaking engagements** | Elevate personal brand, refine communication/presence, expand professional network, drive confidence and executive polish | Global Internal Events, Red Hot Topic Events, Red Hat 101 |
| **1:1 coaching from leadership development professional** | Accelerated self-awareness, targeted behavioral change, personalized development plan | Red Hat Career Coaching Program |
| **Cross-functional project or critical initiative leadership** | Test leadership in ambiguity, demonstrate business impact, build reputation and influence | Internal Gigs Program |
| **Senior leader networking, connections, and exposure** | Direct feedback/coaching, realistic view of senior leadership complexity/expectations | Link Up, Global EA Roster |
| **Executive sponsorship** | Advocacy and championing, increased visibility and access, understanding executive-level decision making | Sponsorship Program |
| **Inclusion in Red Hat & IBM signature events** | Value contribution, sense of belonging, increase business knowledge | Quarterly Day of Learning, Red Hat Summit, Career Week, Manager Day, We Are Red Hat Week |

### Movement readiness eligibility matrix

| Movement type | High Impact | Successful | Evolving | Low | HiPo |
|--------------|-------------|------------|----------|-----|------|
| Promotion in current role | Eligible | Not eligible | Not eligible | Not eligible | Eligible at accelerated pace |
| Promotion outside current role | Eligible | Could be eligible upon review | Not eligible | Not eligible | Eligible at accelerated pace, given priority |
| Lateral move outside current role | Eligible | Eligible | Could be eligible (if poor role fit, per People Team) | Not eligible | Eligible, given priority for open roles |
| Not well placed | N/A | N/A | Typically falls here | Typically falls here | N/A |

### Manager actions by movement readiness

| Movement readiness | Manager action |
|-------------------|----------------|
| **Ready to be promoted in current role** | In Salary Adjustment Cycle in Workday, update compensation and new job profile. If budget constraints, keep rating unchanged to prioritize in next cycle |
| **Ready for lateral/promotion outside current role** | If role identified and approved: submit Change Job. If not: leverage Internal Mobility Source page and Workday Internal Job Hub |
| **Continue growing** | Create development plan using 3 Es model: 70% experience, 20% exposure (includes mentoring/coaching), 10% education |
| **Not well placed** | Consider role realignment. If remaining in current role: high-touch management with frequent 1:1s, coaching, and actions to move from this rating |

### Growth sub-questions by movement readiness

- **Ready for lateral/promotion move:** What roles, departments, functions are they interested in?
- **Continue growing:** What in-role development? Will they take on more responsibilities? New experiences aligned to aspirations?
- **Not well placed:** Skill-role misalignment? What other roles may fit?

### 3 Es Development Model

Development actions should be identified using Experience / Exposure / Education (70/20/10):
- [3 Es Model Reference](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/169VXf3JU2DjhZRgaE6UlEmbU5IFNL7kD-7MfUfYx6h4/)

### Manager commitment question

"As a manager, how can you support your associate? What actions will you take to help ensure your associate continues to grow and develop so they may achieve their career aspirations?"

## 7. Talent Calibration

Process where managers discuss and align proposed ratings and talent actions with their manager and peer managers.

### Preparation

- Think through supporting examples for each associate's ratings
- Ensure fair and equitable assessment
- Must click "Submit" for all talent assessments in Workday for data to populate into the Launch Talent Calibration report

**Managers of Managers also:**
- Review if there is differentiation in performance across the team
- Review associates proposed for promotions against criteria

### During calibration (4 parts)

1. Review performance and high potential designator
2. Review associates identified for promotion in current role
3. Review associates identified as ready to move
4. Discuss talent actions

**Important:** Use "Save for Later" during calibration (allows changes). Do NOT click "Approve" (locks the assessment).

### After calibration

- Consider performance ratings while allocating rewards
- Action on "ready for promotion" in upcoming compensation cycle
- Encourage/support movement for associates ready for lateral/promotion moves outside current role
- Partner with Global People Relations on Low Performers
- For HiPo: action on accelerated development plans, consider succession planning
- Communicate performance ratings to associates in next 1:1 check-in
- Reference: [Communicating Performance Ratings Guide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1XUHTcsnTbPrbbGlCKIrQE6L7SHdNUF_dltbyj1vfIuI/)
