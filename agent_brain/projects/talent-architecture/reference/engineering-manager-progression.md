## Job description summaries

**Associate Manager (M2):** Supports the execution of team objectives and goals. Provides direction to team members according to established processes, offering coaching and mentorship to junior associates. Exercises discretion and evaluative judgment on semi-routine issues of limited complexity. Plays a pivotal role in resource planning, performance management, and team output.

**Manager (M3):** Responsible for managing the tactical work plans that align engineering teams to department objectives. Involves recommending changes to policies and processes and managing team resources to meet goals. Exercises more discretion, judgment, and problem-solving for moderate complexity tasks, requiring mentorship for Associate Managers and engineers.

**Senior Manager (M4):** Sets operational objectives for their teams, delegating tasks to ensure the successful achievement of medium-term goals. Pivotal in translating strategic direction into tactical work and guiding engineering teams in resolving complex issues. Responsible for mentoring multiple teams and contributing to the department's strategy and vision.

**Director (M5):** Plays a critical role in executing long-term strategies that align engineering efforts with organizational objectives. Oversees several multidisciplinary teams, including senior managers, ensuring that business goals are met. Uses extensive judgment and expertise to address highly complex issues, with a significant influence on the company's strategy.

**Senior Director (M6):** Responsible for leading the strategic vision and direction of the engineering function, ensuring alignment with company-wide goals. Leads complex and cross-functional teams, establishing long-term strategies that shape the company's future. As a recognized thought leader, instrumental in solving critical industry-wide challenges, guiding talent strategies, and driving innovation.

## Core responsibilities by level

### 1. Goal achievement and direction

| M2 | M3 | M4 | M5 | M6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lead the team in achieving established goals, providing day-to-day direction | Create and manage tactical work plans to meet departmental goals | Develop and manage medium-term objectives for multiple teams | Oversee the execution of long-term strategic initiatives across multiple teams | Lead the development and communication of long-term engineering strategies |

### 2. Team leadership and management

| M2 | M3 | M4 | M5 | M6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lead performance and pay reviews, ensuring constructive feedback | Lead team in achieving objectives while guiding Associate Managers and senior ICs | Translate strategy into tactical plans, driving successful execution | Manage senior managers and high-level contributors for functional and cross-functional success | Oversee complex, multi-disciplinary teams, ensuring alignment with global objectives |

### 3. Problem solving and issue resolution

| M2 | M3 | M4 | M5 | M6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Collaborate across functions to meet team objectives | Address non-routine, moderately complex issues that impact engineering functions | Oversee multiple teams of managers and senior contributors, ensuring they meet operational goals | Solve highly complex, ambiguous problems with significant organizational impact | Anticipate and resolve critical issues impacting medium to long-term cross-functional goals |

### 4. Innovation and process improvement

| M2 | M3 | M4 | M5 | M6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solve semi-routine issues, applying judgment within defined practices | Lead innovation in team processes, influencing short-term outcomes | Address and anticipate complex issues affecting teams across the engineering function | Ensure the translation of long-term strategy into actionable plans | Collaborate with external stakeholders and industry leaders to lead company-wide initiatives |

### 5. Budget, relationships, and strategy

| M2 | M3 | M4 | M5 | M6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ensure compliance with team-level budgets and contribute to financial objectives | Maintain productive relationships with external and internal stakeholders | Contribute to the broader organizational strategy by collaborating with senior leadership | Build external relationships and represent the company as an industry expert | Lead talent development initiatives, succession planning, and leadership coaching |

### 6. Strategic advisory (M6 only)

Act as a key advisor for strategic decision-making, contributing to the corporate vision.

### Key level transitions for managers

**M2 to M3 shift:**
- From "team goals within established processes" to "tactical work plans aligned to department objectives"
- From "semi-routine issues" to "non-routine, moderately complex issues"
- From "collaborate across functions" to "lead innovation in team processes"
- Skill shift: adds Process Improvement and Cross-Functional Collaboration

**M3 to M4 shift:**
- From "tactical work plans" to "operational objectives and medium-term goals"
- From "own team" to "multiple teams of managers and senior contributors"
- From "influencing short-term outcomes" to "translating strategic direction into tactical work"
- From "mentoring engineers" to "mentoring multiple teams and contributing to strategy"
- Skill shift: Leadership becomes primary; adds Resource Management, Cross-Functional Leadership

**M4 to M5 shift:**
- From "medium-term objectives" to "long-term strategic initiatives"
- From "multiple teams" to "several multidisciplinary teams including senior managers"
- From "complex issues" to "highly complex, ambiguous problems with significant organizational impact"
- From "contributing to strategy" to "executing long-term strategies aligned to organizational objectives"
- Skill shift: adds Strategic Vision, Industry Expertise; Cross-Functional Leadership becomes central
