## Job Description Summaries

### Level 3 - Senior Software Quality Engineer

The Senior Software Quality Engineer exercises good judgment and is responsible for working with minimal instruction, making sound decisions to resolve moderately complex issues related to automated and manual testing. This role requires advanced expertise in designing and developing test environments and maintaining test suites for complex systems, in collaboration with cross-functional teams.

### Level 4 - Principal Software Quality Engineer

The Principal Software Quality Engineer is responsible for leading the resolution of varied and non-routine complex testing activities. This role exercises expert judgment and provides creative solutions to resolve complex testing issues, while also mentoring and providing guidance to newer team members. The Principal Software Quality Engineer may coordinate activities of other team members (as a team lead) to ensure the successful completion of projects and assignments.

### Level 5 - Senior Principal Software Quality Engineer

The Senior Principal Software Quality Engineer is responsible for setting the mid-term strategic direction of quality assurance activities. This role exercises expert judgment and business acumen to resolve highly complex issues impacting critical projects, relationships, and business processes. The Senior Principal Software Quality Engineer may lead the activities of other professional staff, serving as an expert regional or functional subject matter expert.

## Responsibilities by Level

### Level 3 - Senior Software Quality Engineer

1. Design, develop, and maintain complex automated test environments.
2. Conduct in-depth analysis of test results, troubleshooting issues that arise.
3. Mentor junior team members on test strategies and best practices.
4. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to enhance test tools and automation frameworks.
5. Participate in the review and improvement of testing methodologies and processes.
6. Implement and enhance continuous integration and deployment pipelines for seamless software delivery.
7. Demonstrates advanced usage of cloud-based infrastructure and management tools for test environments.
8. Proactively leverage AI technologies to streamline workflows, simplify processes, and enhance overall efficiency.
9. Proactively utilize and experiment with AI-assisted tools and technologies, such as code generation, auto-completion, and intelligent suggestions, to accelerate development cycles, enhance code quality, and support test design, automation, and peer review throughout the development lifecycle.

### Level 4 - Principal Software Quality Engineer

1. Lead the design and development of complex test automation frameworks and testing strategies.
2. Coordinate the activities of a team of engineers to ensure successful test delivery and bug resolution.
3. Apply creative problem-solving to resolve complex bugs and system integration issues.
4. Mentor and provide guidance to new team members on advanced testing practices.
5. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to enhance test coverage and ensure product quality.
6. Design, implement, and manage continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD) pipelines and design best practices for CI/CD, cloud infrastructure and automation.
7. Advocate and ensure customer-focused testing by incorporating real-world usage scenarios and patterns.
8. Identify risks and contribute to mitigation planning.
9. Proactively leverage AI technologies to streamline workflows, simplify processes, and enhance overall efficiency.
10. Proactively utilize and experiment with AI-assisted tools and technologies, such as code generation, auto-completion, and intelligent suggestions, to accelerate development cycles, enhance code quality, and support test design, automation, and peer review throughout the development lifecycle.

### Level 5 - Senior Principal Software Quality Engineer

1. Develop and implement mid-term quality engineering strategies aligned with business goals.
2. Lead the activities of professional staff in resolving critical quality issues.
3. Serve as a subject matter expert in quality engineering practices, both regionally and functionally.
4. Apply business acumen to prioritize and resolve highly complex testing and product quality challenges.
5. Mentor peers and provide expert guidance on quality engineering processes and strategies.
6. Design and implement robust continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines that meet enterprise-level standards.
7. Architect and manage scalable, secure, and cost-effective cloud environments.
8. Identify risks and implement risk mitigation plans.
9. Proactively leverage AI technologies to streamline workflows, simplify processes, and enhance overall efficiency.
10. Proactively utilize and experiment with AI-assisted tools and technologies, such as code generation, auto-completion, and intelligent suggestions, to accelerate development cycles, enhance code quality, and support test design, automation, and peer review throughout the development lifecycle.
