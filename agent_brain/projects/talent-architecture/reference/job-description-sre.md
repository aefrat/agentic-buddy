## Job Description Summaries

### Level 3 - Senior Site Reliability Engineer

The Senior Site Reliability Engineer is responsible for driving the reliability, performance, and scalability of services with minimal instruction. This role involves tackling non-routine assignments and resolving moderately complex issues that directly impact the service's stability and effectiveness. The Senior Site Reliability Engineer applies a deep understanding of software and systems engineering principles to design and implement solutions that enhance service reliability. This position requires good judgment and the ability to prioritize work effectively while contributing to the overall goals of the SRE team and organization.

### Level 4 - Principal Site Reliability Engineer

The Principal Site Reliability Engineer plays a crucial role in ensuring the availability, reliability, and performance of critical services at scale. This role is responsible for managing complex systems and solving intricate problems that have a significant impact on service quality and stability. The Principal Site Reliability Engineer leverages extensive experience in software and systems engineering to automate operations, reduce toil, and lead continuous improvement across the service lifecycle. This engineer works with minimal instruction, demonstrating expert judgment and decision-making capabilities while managing non-routine assignments. Additionally, the role involves collaborating with cross-functional teams and influencing technical direction to enhance system robustness.

### Level 5 - Senior Principal Site Reliability Engineer

The Senior Principal Site Reliability Engineer serves as a pivotal leader in driving the success of critical projects and initiatives aligned with Red Hat's strategic business goals. This role is responsible for setting and achieving mid-term objectives, leveraging deep expertise in both software and systems engineering. As a team lead or regional expert, the Senior Principal Site Reliability Engineer guides other professionals, mentors colleagues, and provides strategic direction to enhance service reliability and performance. This position requires a high level of advanced judgment, business acumen, and the ability to resolve highly complex issues that have a significant impact on key business processes and valuable relationships.

## Responsibilities by Level

### Level 3 - Senior Site Reliability Engineer

1. Lead the development and implementation of robust code and automation scripts to improve service reliability and scalability.
2. Conduct thorough code reviews and testing processes to ensure the highest quality standards in the codebase.
3. Work to solve moderately complex issues, making decisions that impact the service's reliability and performance.
4. Mentor and guide junior engineers, fostering a collaborative environment focused on continuous improvement.
5. Engage in a regular on-call rotation, taking responsibility for critical incidents and ensuring timely resolution.
6. Lead incident response and postmortem processes, implementing solutions to prevent recurrence of issues.
7. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to design, develop, and refine SRE tools and systems that support service objectives.
8. Take ownership of tasks and projects, prioritizing them according to their impact on service health and team goals.

### Level 4 - Principal Site Reliability Engineer

1. Lead the development of code and automation scripts to optimize the scalability, reliability, and performance of services.
2. Conduct thorough code reviews and implement best practices in software development to maintain a high-quality codebase.
3. Mentor and guide junior engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement within the team.
4. Design and implement advanced monitoring and alerting systems to proactively detect and resolve issues.
5. Coordinate and lead complex incident response procedures, ensuring timely resolution and thorough postmortems.
6. Interface with internal stakeholders and external cloud providers to architect, build, and maintain fault-tolerant systems.
7. Manage large-scale, distributed systems, focusing on minimizing downtime and improving system resilience.
8. Participate in an on-call rotation and provide leadership during critical incidents to ensure 24x7x365 production support.
9. Lead the continuous enhancement of the SRE team's processes, tools, and methodologies to support the evolving needs of the service.

### Level 5 - Senior Principal Site Reliability Engineer

1. Lead the design and implementation of scalable, reliable, and secure systems that meet the evolving needs of the business.
2. Set strategic goals for the SRE team, aligning with Red Hat's business objectives, and lead initiatives to achieve those goals.
3. Provide technical leadership in incident response, root cause analysis, and implementation of long-term solutions to prevent recurrence.
4. Mentor and develop team members, fostering an environment of continuous learning and technical excellence.
5. Act as a liaison between SRE teams and other engineering groups, ensuring seamless integration of systems and services.
6. Lead complex, multi-region deployments and optimizations, ensuring minimal downtime and maximum performance.
7. Lead the adoption of industry best practices in site reliability, automation, and operational excellence across the organization.
8. Oversee the continuous improvement of monitoring, alerting, and response systems to enhance overall service reliability.
9. Represent the SRE team in executive-level discussions, providing insights and recommendations to influence key decisions.
