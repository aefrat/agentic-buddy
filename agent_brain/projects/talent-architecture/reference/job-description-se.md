## Job Description Summaries

### Level 3 - Senior Software Engineer

The Senior Software Engineer is a technical leader who designs, implements, and ensures the quality of complex software solutions to solve our customers' most pressing challenges. This position exists to provide technical leadership that champions a customer-first mindset and a culture of quality within the engineering team. As a mentor and guide, a primary function is to establish and enforce quality standards, ensuring the team delivers scalable, high-quality systems that create measurable value for our users.

### Level 4 - Principal Software Engineer

The Principal Software Engineer is a technical authority who leads the design of systems that involve multiple components and coordinates across teams, setting a technical direction that anticipates future customer needs and establishes the standard for software quality. This role is responsible for ensuring the integrity and long-term quality of our software, ensuring it empowers our customers to deliver on their goals.

### Level 5 - Senior Principal Software Engineer

The Senior Principal Software Engineer is a recognized expert who drives innovation across teams to define the technical strategy to solve business-critical problems for our customers. This position exists to provide leadership that connects our technology capabilities with opportunities to create significant customer value through superior software quality. The primary function is to architect large-scale, future-proof systems that not only solve current customer challenges but also set a new standard for reliability and performance, ensuring our products are built on a foundation of technical excellence.

## Responsibilities by Level

### Level 3 - Senior Software Engineer

1. **Technical Impact:** Leads the design and development of software solutions for a subsystem or component.
2. **Quality:** Owns the quality of their code and contributes to testing frameworks.
3. **Community:** Recognized as an active contributor to internal tooling or upstream projects, serving as a role model for community engagement. Often serves as a project reviewer or maintainer.
4. **Mentoring:** Mentors and coaches other engineers.
5. **Business Impact:** Owns and delivers features with clear business impact, to deliver end user value.
6. **Technical Practices:** Supports and implements the adoption of new tools within their team.
7. **AI Tools:** Identifies and resolves moderately complex issues by implementing AI-powered agents to automate non-routine workflows and enhance existing team processes.
8. **SDLC:** Champions and helps refine the team's SDLC, identifying areas for improvement and mentoring junior engineers on best practices to ensure a predictable and high-quality development process.

### Level 4 - Principal Software Engineer

1. **Technical Impact:** Leads the design and development of software solutions for features that cross multiple subsystems or components.
2. **Quality:** Establishes, maintains, and monitors testing practices involving multiple components and teams.
3. **Community:** Acts as a key representative and leader within the community, advocating for internal or external customer needs, while aligning products with the community direction.
4. **Mentoring:** Across teams, coaches and mentors senior engineers.
5. **Business Impact:** Owns and delivers technical initiatives with visible business impact, enabling the entire team to deliver value to the end user.
6. **Technical Practices:** Drives the adoption of new tools within a set of teams.
7. **AI Tools:** Evaluates and introduces new AI-driven methodologies that resolve complex issues and improve Engineering efficiency.
8. **SDLC:** Leads the definition and implementation of the SDLC for complex multi-component systems, ensuring that the process scales effectively and aligns with the needs of multiple collaborating teams.

### Level 5 - Senior Principal Software Engineer

1. **Technical Impact:** Drives the technical strategy and design of software solutions across multiple subsystems, influencing the overall architecture.
2. **Quality:** Establishes, maintains, and monitors testing practices for large-scale systems involving multiple teams. Follows established architectural patterns that ensure reliability.
3. **Community:** Drives innovation by leading significant product-area initiatives with a community-first mindset. Often participates across multiple communities, fosters and monitors community health, engages in industry and internal working groups, and engages in the evaluation of new communities.
4. **Mentoring:** Across organizations, coaches and mentors principal engineers and role models mentorship for the organization.
5. **Business Impact:** Owns and drives technical initiatives across the organization recognizing which pieces flow together to deliver value to the end user.
6. **Technical Practices:** Drives the adoption of new tools within the organization.
7. **AI Tools:** Drives the strategy and best practices for integrating advanced AI ecosystems to automate large-scale systems.
8. **SDLC:** Drives the evolution of the SDLC within the organization, introducing new methodologies and best practices that improve the efficiency and effectiveness of multiple teams contributing to a large-scale system.

## Job Skills by Level

### Level 3 - Senior Software Engineer

1. **Technical Acumen:** A go-to person for technical challenges within the team. Designs and implements solutions for complex features. Owns the quality of their code and contributes significantly to the team's test automation.
2. **Quality Management:** Drives quality within the team by designing and building robust automated testing frameworks for complex features and mentoring others on testing best practices.
3. **System Design:** Designs and implements solutions for complex features, making trade-off decisions within the existing architecture.
4. **Communication:** Clearly communicates complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences. Produces persuasive design documents.
5. **Collaboration:** Fosters a collaborative environment within the team while leading the design of a subsystem or component.
6. **Leadership:** A technical leader on the team. Coaches other engineers. Sets a high standard for engineering excellence.
7. **Business Impact:** Makes technical trade-offs based on a clear understanding of which solutions will deliver the most business value to customers.
8. **Continuous Learning:** Continuously learns and experiments with new technologies. Shares their knowledge with the team.
9. **Influence:** Influences the technical direction of their team and adjacent teams by building consensus in broader discussions with internal customers or through active participation in external communities.
10. **Knowledge Sharing:** Shares expertise by producing comprehensive design documents, writing blog posts, and mentoring others on documentation and communication best practices.

### Level 4 - Principal Software Engineer

1. **Technical Acumen:** Leads the design and implementation of multi-component systems that span across teams. An expert in multiple areas of the tech stack. Designs and builds core components of test automation.
2. **Quality Management:** Establishes and scales quality standards by architecting and implementing cutting-edge, automated testing frameworks that are leveraged by multiple teams across the organization.
3. **System Design:** Leads the design of complex systems that involve multiple components and teams.
4. **Communication:** Effectively communicates with leadership and stakeholders. Presents complex system plans in a clear and compelling way.
5. **Collaboration:** Builds strong cross-functional relationships to lead the design of features that successfully span multiple subsystems or components.
6. **Leadership:** Leads and sets the technical direction for complex multi-component systems. Coaches senior engineers.
7. **Business Impact:** Proposes and advocates for technical solutions that deliver visible business impact.
8. **Continuous Learning:** Advocates the adoption of new technologies and patterns across multiple teams.
9. **Influence:** Acts as a recognized technical leader who influences roadmaps and drives decisions that serve internal developer customers or align with the direction of key upstream communities.
10. **Knowledge Sharing:** Shares expertise by producing comprehensive design documents, writing blog posts, and mentoring others on documentation and communication best practices. Presents at technical conferences.

### Level 5 - Senior Principal Software Engineer

1. **Technical Acumen:** Leads the design and implementation of large-scale systems that span multiple teams. An expert in multiple areas of the tech stack. Evolves test automation to support the quality strategy of the organization.
2. **Quality Management:** Owns the quality and reliability strategy for large-scale systems by designing sophisticated testing methodologies and establishing the architectural patterns that ensure resilience and quality at scale.
3. **System Design:** Leads the design of large-scale systems across many teams. Architects scalable and fault-tolerant systems that anticipate customer needs.
4. **Communication:** Communicates complex, multi-faceted technical strategies effectively to leaders and engineers across the organization to create alignment and drive execution.
5. **Collaboration:** Collaborates with senior leadership and technical experts across teams to define technical strategy and influence the overall architecture. Connects with customers to gain empathy for pain points to solve.
6. **Leadership:** A technical leader across large-scale systems spanning many teams. Mentors principal engineers.
7. **Business Impact:** Drives technical initiatives with financial impact across many teams.
8. **Continuous Learning:** Guides the adoption of industry trends and emerging technologies.
9. **Influence:** Drives significant technical change within their organization by building broad consensus for complex strategies that influence critical internal platforms or strategic upstream communities.
10. **Knowledge Sharing:** Shares expertise by producing comprehensive architectural design documents, writing blog posts, and mentoring teams on documentation and communication best practices. Frequently presents at technical conferences, often to larger audiences.
