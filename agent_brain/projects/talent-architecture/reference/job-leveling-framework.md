# Red Hat Job Leveling Framework (Radford-based)

Source: Job Leveling Framework PDF, February 2025.

Job leveling defines and standardizes the nature of work performed by roles at each level across the organization. It helps associates and managers understand what's expected, how they fit into the organization, and what they need to do to progress.

## Three career tracks

**Management** - Contributing through others to drive results. Responsible for driving resource decisions and applying Red Hat's Manager Expectations to deliver on company objectives and create an environment of belonging, respect, and mutual support. Makes hiring and termination decisions, manages conflict and performance, conducts performance evaluations and pay reviews, and nurtures a culture of shared purpose, integrity, respect, and innovation.

**Professional** - Applying specialist expertise to deliver results. Contributes through the application of technical proficiency and specialist expertise. Responsibilities often require a progressive level of advanced decision-making and collaboration. Creative and innovative in approach, uses independent thought to develop and maintain collaborative work standards. At higher levels, applies expertise to drive the business forward.

**Support** - Supporting the delivery of Red Hat results. Completes specific tasks or activities that support the delivery and implementation of Red Hat's solutions and services. Typically contributes by undertaking individual responsibilities as part of a broader team, from close supervision to working independently on assignments that require initiative to resolve.

## Three dimensions

Dimensions describe the nature of work performed at each level - the "what." Competencies describe the behaviors expected - the "how."

### Scope
- Functional and geographic reach of the job
- Breadth and degree of responsibility
- Nature of guidance and/or management provided to others
- **Considerations:** Size of team/functional oversight, degree of personal/team/local/global ownership, career level of associates managed, breadth of work accountable for, level of guidance required or independence exercised

### Complexity
- Nature of work or role in projects
- Judgment, analysis, or creativity required to understand or solve problems
- Knowledge and skills of core principles or concepts within specialty area
- Nature and level of relationships within or outside the organization, degree of influence and collaboration
- **Considerations:** Types of problems solved at this level, extent novel vs. existing approaches needed, areas of development/proficiency/expertise, level of associates interacted with, tone and intent of those interactions

### Impact
- Degree and scope of accountability for results
- Contribution to strategy
- The level of and impact decisions have on the business or team
- **Considerations:** Types of decisions made independently, lasting impact of decisions, significance to the future of the team/business unit/Red Hat overall

## Progression descriptors (low to high)

| Description | Low | | | | | High |
|-------------|-----|---|---|---|---|------|
| Impact and reach | Minimal impact | Limited impact (<3 months) | Short term impact (3-6 months) | Medium impact (1 year) | Medium to long term (1-3 years) | Long term impact (3+ years) |
| Problems solved | Minimally complex | Limited complexity | Moderately complex | Complex | Highly complex | Significantly complex |
| Expertise | Entry/new role | Intermediate/developing | Career/full knowledge | Advanced/breadth of knowledge | Expert/broad or unique knowledge | Principal/expert in industry |
| Daily responsibilities | Routine | Semi-routine | Non-routine | Varied and non-routine | Consistently non-routine | Ambiguous in nature |
| Interactions | Team | Team(s)/discipline | Disciplines | Function (CLT organization) | Cross-functional | Large region/global |

## Management Track (M2-M6)

### Scope

| | M2 - Associate Manager | M3 - Manager | M4 - Senior Manager | M5 - Director | M6 - Senior Director |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Objectives | Receives assignments as objectives with goals and the process by which to meet goals | Receives assignments as objectives and determines how to use resources to meet schedule and goals | Establishes operational objectives and medium term (annual) targets, delegating goals and assignments to the team | Responsible for executing and enabling long-term strategy, providing context, and making final decisions on business matters to ensure achievement of functional goals | Responsible for establishing and communicating the long term strategy that aligns functional and Red Hat goals |
| Direction | Provides direction to associates according to established processes, policies and management guidance | Recommends and establishes changes to processes and policies that affect their own area using business context | Contributes to the development of strategy, executing and enabling it to support the team in achieving functional goals | Partners with members of the leadership team to establish medium to long-term strategic plans and operational goals | Applies an enterprise mindset and comprehensive knowledge of the market, to establish long-term strategic plans |
| Management span | Provides daily direction to a Red Hat associates (typically entry, developing level) | Provides direct management for team of Red Hat associates, may include Associate Managers; may manage team or project within a matrixed environment | Provides direct and indirect management for multiple teams of managers and experienced individual contributors; may include multiple teams or projects within a matrixed environment | Directs several multi-disciplinary teams through Senior Managers and high level individual contributors; may include cross functional management of teams/projects in a matrixed environment | Recognized as an influential leader of Senior Managers, Directors and high level individual contributors. Considered a functional thought leader |
| People responsibility | Responsible for performance and pay reviews, hiring and resource planning for own team | Responsible for performance and pay reviews, hiring and resource planning for own team, as well as reviewing plans of reporting managers | Responsible for performance and pay reviews, hiring and resource planning for own team, as well as reviewing plans of direct, indirect and dotted line reporting managers | Responsible for performance and pay reviews, hiring and resource planning for own team, as well as ensuring people management decisions across the function align to functional and talent strategies | In addition to full people management responsibilities, responsible for the development of talent strategies, succession planning, and leadership within their organization |
| Reports to | | Typically reports to a Director or Senior Director | Typically reports to VPs or Senior Directors | Typically reports to VP or SVP corporate leaders | |

### Complexity

| | M2 | M3 | M4 | M5 | M6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Issues | Works on semi-routine issues of limited complexity that require evaluative judgment of a range of factors | Works on non-routine issues of moderate complexity where needs and solutions may be ambiguous | Works on resolving and anticipating complex issues that impact multiple teams across related Red Hat areas | Works on solving highly complex, unique and ambiguous issues which may impact success of attaining medium to long-term functional targets | Works on anticipating, defining and solving significantly complex and critical issues which impact success of attaining medium to long term - cross-functional targets |
| Knowledge | Applies general knowledge of area's policies and practices to independently complete standardized processes | Applies knowledge of area(s) of expertise to understand functional strategies and translate direction into tactical work plans | Transfers and integrates advanced knowledge to direct expert who takes ownership of Red Hat practices | Recognized as a company expert who takes ownership of cross-functional teams across different, but related, specialties | Recognized both internally and externally as an expert in the field/industry able to drive enterprise-wide initiatives |
| Interactions | Interacts with peers across disciplines to ensure attainment of operational team targets | Interacts with functional management across multiple disciplines to achieve matter experts to achieve departmental objectives | Collaborates with Senior Red Hat leadership and subject matter experts to achieve functional objectives | Collaborates with internal stakeholders to drive business strategy and achieve cross-functional objectives | Collaborates with key internal and external stakeholders and leadership teams to enable and execute strategies and achieve regional or global business results |
| External | | May build productive external working relationships with customers where role requires | Builds productive external working relationships with customers where role requires | Acts as an expert advisor to Red Hat or customers on matters related to business success | Acts as an expert advisor to Red Hat and industry. Builds external alliances with partners and customers to enhance Red Hat's image |
| Experience | Typically requires two to five years of related operational experience | Typically requires five to eight years of operational experience | Typically requires eight years of operational experience and two years of supervisory experience of managers, skilled specialists and established individual contributors | Typically requires ten years of operational experience and five years of supervisory experience of experienced managers and subject matter experts | Typically requires twelve years of operational experience and eight years of supervisory experience of directors, managers and subject matter experts |

### Impact

| | M2 | M3 | M4 | M5 | M6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contribution | Contributes to the achievement of team objectives through influencing output of team | Contributes to the achievement of functional objectives through influencing outputs of respective team(s) | Contributes to the interpretation of strategic plans and guides the translation into specific functional objectives | Contributes to development of business objectives by translating corporate strategy into functional plans and targets, guiding and enabling execution | Contributes to development of corporate vision, translating Red Hat's objectives into long-term functional strategy, driving execution |
| Decisions | Decisions impact short-term team performance, with limited direct impact on Red Hat's long-term goals | Decisions impact the achievement of discipline's short-term goals, providing oversight of managers and their direct reports | Decisions impact the achievement of discipline's medium-term goals | Decisions impact the function's achievement of medium to long-term results and strategy | Decisions impact functional and cross-functional achievement of medium to long-term results and strategy |
| Innovation | Provides feedback on processes and procedures that impact achievement of team goals | Implements processes and procedures that affect achievement of team goals; begins to suggest innovative alternatives | Drives innovation of operational direction and tactical activities that affect achievement of functional goals | Implements current year strategy with innovation and agility to execute and achieve cross-functional goals | Translates corporate vision into agile and strategic business plans, guiding and enabling management execution for long term success |
| Financial | Contributes to financial results and operational budgets through daily direction of associates | Contributes to financial results and operational budgets through influencing outputs of respective team(s) | Contributes to financial results and operational budgets through the achievement of functional objectives | Provides input into the annual budgeting process. Has significant impact on financial results and achievement of objectives | May be accountable for the operational budget of multiple functions or a global business area; or profitability of external relationships |

## Professional Track (Level 1-6)

### Scope

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 | Level 5 | Level 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Independence | Responsible for following detailed instructions and standardized procedures to complete assigned tasks and meet deadlines | Responsible for following general instructions and working independently to prioritize work and meet deadlines | Responsible for working independently and with minimal instruction to prioritize work and meet deadlines | Responsible for working independently to set and achieve objectives tied to functional targets and goals | Responsible for working independently to set and achieve mid-term objectives aligned to Red Hat company business goals | Responsible for creating and guiding functional plans based on long-term Red Hat business goals, industry trends and technological evolution |
| Delivery | Supports activities aligned to established processes and priorities of own area | Delivers on assigned activities aligned to established processes and priorities of own team | Delivers on/Enhances existing processes or products to support business priorities | Proposes new techniques and methods based on business context that have impact within the function | Drives vision and innovation, determining best procedures to be used on assignments that have a cross-functional impact | Drives vision and innovation of new products and enhances the reputation of Red Hat |
| Team lead | | | Coordinates activities of other team members (team lead) to ensure successful completion of projects and assignments | Coordinates activities of other team members (team lead) to ensure successful completion of projects and assignments | Leads the activities of other professional staff (team lead), serving as expert regional or functional resource | Leads activities of broader team of professional staff (team lead), serving as expert regional or global resource |
| Mentoring | | | Mentors and provides guidance and advice to new team members | Mentors and provides guidance and advice to new team members | Mentors and provides guidance and advice to colleagues in their own discipline | Mentors and provides guidance to colleagues beyond their own discipline or across the business; demonstrates role-model qualities |

### Complexity

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 | Level 5 | Level 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Judgment | Follows routine procedures to complete standard assignments and resolve issues of minimal complexity | Exercises judgment to work on routine and semi-routine assignments and resolves issues of limited complexity | Demonstrates good judgment to work independently on non-routine assignments and resolves moderately complex issues that impact own area | Applies independent judgment to work on varied and non-routine projects and resolves complex issues with creative solutions that impact overall function | Exercises independent judgment and business acumen to work on and resolve highly complex issues that impact success of critical projects, valuable relationships and key business processes | Exercises significant judgment and interpretative understanding to work on and resolve critical issues that will have an impact on long-term regional or global business success |
| Expertise | Acquires experience by completing assigned activities and helping fellow team members | Applies professional experience to take ownership of assigned activities, resolve problems and support team initiatives | Recognized as an experienced professional able to advise less experienced peers and solve a variety of issues | Recognized as an expert within the team or department with extensive mastery of functional, business or technical knowledge | Recognized as a subject matter expert, able to apply technical knowledge and a broad understanding of other disciplines to advise functional leaders and/or customers on strategy | Recognized as a thought leader and technical authority, able to apply advanced knowledge of theories and concepts to advise Red Hat executive leaders, and customers on strategy |
| Relationships | Has stable working relationships within own team | Has productive working relationships within own team and peers on related teams | Has productive working relationships within discipline; has some interaction with senior colleagues | Has productive working relationships within function and leads cooperative efforts among teams to deliver results. Interacts with senior colleagues and customers frequently | Has formal networks across Red Hat to influence priorities and objectives both internally and with customers; consistently reinforces "One Red Hat" mindset | Has formal networks with key internal decision makers; builds external alliances with partners and customers to enhance the image of Red Hat as a global innovator |
| Experience | Typically, less than two years of related professional experience | Typically, a minimum of two years of related experience | Typically, a minimum of five years of related professional experience | Typically, a minimum of eight years of related professional experience | Typically, a minimum of twelve years of related professional experience | Typically, a minimum of fifteen plus years of related experience |

### Impact

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 | Level 5 | Level 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contribution | Contributes to the achievement of team's short-term goals through completion of assigned tasks | Contributes to the achievement of team goals through completion of tasks | Contributes to the achievement of team and discipline's goals through completion of tasks | Contributes to the achievement of functional goals through operational direction and tactical activities | Activities directly impact function's achievement of medium to long-term results and strategy | Activities directly impact Red Hat's achievement of long-term regional or global results and strategy |
| Decisions | Decisions have minimal impact on work of immediate team | Decisions have limited impact on work of immediate team | Decisions moderately impact work of others on immediate team. May have minimal impact on other internal teams | Decisions impact the achievement of customer, operational, or program objectives | Decisions significantly impact the direction and resource allocation for customer, operational or program objectives | Decisions directly impact and drive business through the development of innovative customer, operational or program objectives and deliverables |
| Project role | | | Works independently or on moderate scope projects | Begins to serve as project lead or team-level expert for complex issues and projects | Consistently serves as project lead and technical subject matter expert for highly complex issues and projects | |

## Support Track (Level 1-5)

### Scope

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Supervision | Follows established procedures to complete own routine activities | Follows established guidelines and delivers own work activities to meet scheduled and prioritized timelines | Receives little supervision and instruction on daily routine work, general guidance on newly introduced semi-routine to non-routine assignments | Receives minimal supervision on routine tasks, jointly determines methods and procedures on new non-routine assignments | Acts independently to determine methods and procedures to complete new non-routine assignments |
| Quality | Work is clearly defined and prioritized, receiving instructions and close manager guidance | Work is clearly defined, receiving instructions from manager on semi-routine assignments | Assures quality of own deliverables/own service level | Accountable for the quality review of deliverables of junior team members | Responsible for coordinating delivery of own work and that of others |
| Team lead | | | Up-skills new hires on operation of standardized tools and resources | May be an informal team lead for junior team members | Team lead of junior team members |

### Complexity

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Judgment | Minimal judgment required; works on problems of minimal complexity | Applies basic judgment to recognize the need for occasional deviation from accepted practice; works on problems of limited complexity | Applies judgment to evaluate and resolve issues of moderate complexity and where necessary deviate from accepted practice | Applies judgment to evaluate and resolve unusual and difficult issues that often require deviation from accepted practice | Applies judgment to objectively assess complex situations, evaluate multiple sometimes conflicting sources of information and implement non-standard solutions |
| Knowledge | Requires basic understanding of company procedures and methods | Requires a general understanding of related company policies and some multi-step processes | Requires broad knowledge of different processes and techniques to complete a wide range of related tasks | Requires strong understanding of practices and procedures in own area to schedule, complete and monitor tasks resourcefully | Requires high degree of practical and technical understanding to complete complex assignments in creative ways. Contributes to the development of techniques and working methods in own area |
| Interactions | Interacts with own team on projects under direction of manager or more experienced associates | Interacts with own team, occasionally colleagues from similar teams within discipline | Interacts with other teams within function | Often interacts with other teams within function or cross-functionally | Interacts with other teams or cross-functionally, potentially on a regional or global basis |
| Experience | Typically, less than one year of related experience | Typically, one to two years of related work experience | Typically, two to four years of related experience | Typically, a minimum of four to six years of related experience | Typically, a minimum of six years of related experience |

### Impact

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Supervision | Receives detailed instructions on all assignments; works under close supervision | Follows established procedures on routine work; requires instructions only on semi-routine assignments | Receives little instruction on semi-routine work; general instructions on non-routine assignments | Determines methods and procedures on new varied and non-routine assignments | Consistently works on complex, issue-driven non-routine assignments, requiring substantial practical experience |
| Error impact | Errors can usually be detected and corrected quickly, normally leading to minimal financial impact | Errors may be detected and corrected but may result in minimal delay and rework with limited financial impact | Anticipates and resolves non-routine issues that may be escalated from junior team members; recognizes when to deviate from standard process. Errors may cause delay and rework with short-term financial impact | Thoroughness and reliability are essential due to moderate discretion in decision making; errors may have moderate financial impact | Thoroughness and reliability are essential due to broad discretion in decision making; errors may have moderate to long-term financial impact |
| Project role | Works independently on basic projects | Works independently or on limited scope projects | Works independently or on moderate scope projects | Begins to serve as project lead or team-level expert for lower-level staff for complex issues and projects | Consistently serves as project lead and technical subject matter expert for highly complex issues and projects |
