📋 1. RHAS Strategic Guide — Summary
The pivot: After Red Hat Summit 2025 lab preparation pain (integration instability, missing features, cross-BU dependencies), the strategy shifted from a comprehensive bundled product to a staged, incremental approach — only leading with products proven to work together. The full bundle remains the visionary end-state, not the opening pitch.

Three-Tier Architecture

Tier 1 — Standalone Builder

  • 🎯 Systems Admins, cost-sensitive orgs
  • 🔧 RHEL + AIB + Language Containers
  • ✅ Max control, portable artifacts, minimal cost
  • ❌ Manual, no CI/CD, not scalable
  • 💡 Entry point; creates the "value vacuum" that sells OCP

Tier 2 — Automotive Suite ⭐ (RHAS)

  • 🎯 Platform Engineers
  • 🔧 OCP + Builder Op + Jumpstarter + Pipelines + GitOps + Keycloak
  • ✅ Integrates existing GitForge — no rip-and-replace
  • ✅ Hardware-in-the-loop testing via Jumpstarter
  • ❌ Multi-vendor complexity, limited governance
  • 💡 The core shipped product

Tier 3 — Unified Environment (IDP)

  • 🎯 CIO/CISO, greenfield projects
  • 🔧 Adds Dev Spaces + Developer Hub + Quay
  • ✅ Single vendor, centralized governance, max productivity
  • ❌ Highest cost, vendor lock-in, dedicated platform eng team required
  • 💡 Long-term "north star" — lead with vision, land as Tier 2

Delivery Model

What ships: IaC (Ansible playbooks), Kubernetes Operators for lifecycle, persona-driven docs.

Release cadence: Dual-track — OS-aligned major releases tied to RHIVOS + continuous delivery for Builder/Jumpstarter via feature gates.

Security Posture

Software supply chain review (especially Jumpstarter dependencies). Principle of least privilege — privileged containers mitigated with guidance now, removed architecturally long-term.
