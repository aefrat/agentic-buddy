Physical AI - RHIVOS BU Initiative Brief

Compiled 2026-07-23 | Sources: AUTOBU-1108, RHIVOS 2.x Planning, RHIVOS+ Presentation, Slack

Executive Summary: RHIVOS+ is a proposed extension of Red Hat In-Vehicle OS that positions RHIVOS as a certified, open source governance platform for Physical AI - AI agents that control physical systems beyond automotive. The initiative targets robotics, drones, industrial automation, and energy systems through a three-layer defense model built on RHIVOS's existing ISO 26262 ASIL-B certified Linux foundation, with NVIDIA OpenShell providing agent sandboxing.

NEW GROWTH Created Jul 17, 2026 by Gadi Glogowski. Early-stage concept with phased roadmap starting Q3 2026.

The Problem

Agentic software is fundamentally different from traditional processes - decisions are made at runtime by an LLM, making behavior non-deterministic and untrusted by default. As AI agents enter safety-critical physical systems (robotics, drones, industrial automation), application-level guardrails alone are insufficient. True enforcement must occur at a privilege boundary the agent cannot cross.

Three requirements for safe agent operation on physical systems:

  1. Containment - The AI agent runs in a partition that cannot interfere with safety-critical functions
  2. Governance - Enforce what the agent can access at the OS level
  3. Validation - The agent produces a plan; that plan is verified against a safety envelope before any physical action

Architecture - Three-Layer Defense Model

Layer 1: RHIVOS+ (Software - Containment, Governance, Validation)

Extends RHIVOS with agent-specific governance and validation within the certified safety architecture. Adds governance within the non-safety (QM) partition where AI agents operate. Enforces architectural separation between agent planning and physical execution.

  • Containment: Kernel-level isolation via 9+ mechanisms (namespaces, cgroups, seccomp, LSMs, SELinux, capabilities, MMU, scheduler policies, Podman/systemd)
  • Governance: NVIDIA OpenShell - programmable agent sandboxes with four policy layers (Filesystem, Network, Process, Inference). Live policy updates with full audit trail
  • Validation: Sense-Plan-Validate-Act architecture. Untrusted plans never reach actuators without validation against a safety envelope (position, velocity, force, authorization)

Certified safety claims: Spatial Isolation (memory boundaries), Temporal Isolation (CPU/memory/resource limits), Resource Isolation (device/filesystem/network access control). ISO 26262 ASIL-B certified.

Layer 2: Safety Controller (Certified Hardware)

Dedicated MCU/ECU independent of all AI software. Per-actuator current/torque/force limits. E-stop circuits. Hardware watchdogs. Certified to ISO 26262 (automotive), IEC 61508 (industrial), ISO 13482 (robotics).

Layer 3: Physical System (Physics)

Motor torque limits (back-EMF), mechanical stops, crush zones, breakaway mechanisms, fuses. Physics is the final enforcer - software cannot override.

NVIDIA OpenShell - Agent Governance Partnership

Red Hat and NVIDIA are collaborating on OpenShell to bring the same agent governance from the datacenter to embedded devices on RHIVOS. OpenShell provides:

  • Programmable sandboxes - "break-safe" environments per agent
  • Granular policy enforcement - filesystem, network, process, and inference layers
  • Gateway - translates policy rules into runtime enforcement decisions
  • Threat mitigation - data exfiltration (network policies), credential theft (Landlock), unauthorized API usage (privacy routing), privilege escalation (seccomp)

Target Markets and Use Cases

Autonomous Forklifts Warehouse/logistics. Plans efficient pick paths, adapts routes in real time. Force, speed, and zone limits enforced by safety controller.
In-Vehicle Voice Assistants Calls vehicle APIs for climate, navigation, media. Cannot actuate steering, braking, or drivetrain without validated plan.
Drone Fleet Management Coordinates flight plans across delivery drone fleets. Geofencing and altitude limits enforced; no-fly zones immutable.
Robotic Arms in Manufacturing Optimizes assembly sequence based on sensor feedback. Torque and force envelopes enforced at hardware level.

Target certifications: ISO 26262 (automotive), IEC 61508 (industrial), ISO 13482 (robotics/personal care).

Observations and Assessment

  • Very early stage. AUTOBU-1108 was created Jul 17 with no child tickets, no assignee, and no linked issues. The Outcome is well-articulated but execution structures are not yet in place.
  • NVIDIA partnership is central. OpenShell is a joint collaboration. NVIDIA's Physical AI market push (Japan investment, Cosmos consortium, Omron engagement) is a strong external driver.
  • Builds on proven RHIVOS foundations. The three-layer defense model leverages RHIVOS's existing ISO 26262 ASIL-B certification, mixed-criticality container isolation, and PREEMPT_RT kernel capabilities. RHIVOS+ is an extension, not a rebuild.
  • Multiple teams already active. Ecosystems Engineering (Chris Custine), edge robotics WG (Luis Arizmendi), research/sim (saypaul + fzdarsky), SSA field (Xantara), and #rockwell-team (Luke Thompson) are all working on related streams.
  • Requirements clarity is the blocker. Phases 2 and 3 (safety policy engine and certified stack) are explicitly blocked on "clear definition of requirements for Physical AI." Phase 1 needs "direction on use cases."
  • Cross-industry certification is ambitious. Spanning ISO 26262 (automotive), IEC 61508 (industrial), and ISO 13482 (robotics) means significant certification effort beyond the existing automotive scope.
  • Active thought leadership play. Luis Arizmendi's blog series, Eclipse working group participation, and conference talks are planned for the concept/Phase 1 timeline.
  • Real customer signals. Omron asking to engage, Xantara/Fertig Motors SSA engagement for on-prem physical AI with cameras, NVIDIA Cosmos consortium partners in Japan manufacturing/robotics.
