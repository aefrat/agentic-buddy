Executive Summary

Total monthly spend across 3 AWS accounts is $26,347 (July 2026) - 32% higher than the estimated $20k. The primary account (ateam) has grown 61% over 5 months ($12.3k to $19.8k), driven by EBS snapshot accumulation ($12k/month, 61% of account). The stage account revealed a ROSA/OCP cluster consuming $2,050/month (79% of that account), with $968/month in provisioned IOPS that may be replaceable by GP3. Eitan's snapshot cleanup (completed early August) should deliver the majority of the 20% reduction target ($5,270/month). Additional savings from IOPS optimization, S3 lifecycle policies, and cleanup of legacy/test resources provide a further buffer.

6. New Findings (Not in Eitan's Plan)

Finding 1: auto-product-build-downstream - 266 TB (~$6,130/month) Critical

This single S3 bucket accounts for nearly all S3 cost in the ateam account. It stores RHIVOS downstream nightly builds plus the 3 latest releases, with versioning enabled. At S3 Standard pricing ($0.023/GB in eu-west-1), 266 TB costs ~$6,130/month.

Recommendation: (a) Review if all 266 TB is current data or accumulated versions. Versioning + no lifecycle = unlimited growth. (b) Add a lifecycle policy to expire old versions after N days. (c) Consider S3 Intelligent-Tiering or Infrequent Access for archive data. Even moving 50% to IA would save ~$1,800/month.

Relates to: Eitan's item #10 (S3 retention review)
